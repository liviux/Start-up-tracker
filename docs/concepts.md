# Concepts

The words the tracker uses, and where each is explained.

**Startup**: one company or project. Each startup has its own targets, company records, media, history and recycle bin, and its own address, `/s/<id>/`. One tracker can hold several. See [Startups](startups.md).

**Sections**: the four areas of a startup, each with a code and a colour:

- **MK**, Marketing: places to launch, list, post or pitch, such as directories, communities and press.
- **PO**, Programs and opportunities: accelerators, competitions, grants with an application, perks.
- **FU**, Funding: investors and funding sources, with commitments and payments.
- **COMP**, Company: the legal entity's setup steps, obligations, deadlines and expenses. See [Company](company.md).

**Target**: one entry in MK, PO or FU, for example one directory, one accelerator or one investor. It has a status, categories, dates, notes, links and a history. You open it in the [target window](fields.md).

**Status**: where a target stands. Statuses belong to four groups, Planned, In progress, Done and Closed, which drive the counts and filters. See [Statuses](statuses.md).

**Categories**: the library that sorts targets in three levels, category, subcategory and type, for each of MK, PO and FU. Each startup turns on the parts it uses. See [Categories](categories.md).

**Links**: connections between two records, such as a target and the program it led to, shown on both. See [Links](links.md). The startup's own links (website, profiles) are something else: they live in the startup profile and the Links menu at the top.

**Campaigns** and **tags**: labels shared by every startup. A campaign groups targets around one push, such as a launch; tags are free labels. See [Extra](extra.md).

**Fields**: the details a target holds, from a registry with help text and examples; you can add your own. See [The target window and fields](fields.md).

**Focus queue**: the targets that need something on a given day ("On this day") or by then ("Due by"), at the top of each section page. See [Marketing, Programs and Funding pages](section-pages.md).

**Prompts** and **AI agents**: prompt templates filled with your startup's details, to paste into a chat or run with an agent on your computer. See [Prompts](prompts.md) and [AI agents](agents.md).

**Backups** and the **recycle bin**: deleted records wait in the recycle bin for 30 days; backups copy the whole database and its media. See [Backups](backups.md).
